## Why I built it

Most AI governance material is dense, jargon-heavy or behind a paywall. Teams need judgement, not a list of regulations to memorise. This course turns the major frameworks into a guided path anyone can follow for free. The interface is in Arabic and English; the Arabic lessons are in progress.

## What it does

- An eight-stage path from first concepts to a working governance output.
- Frameworks covered: the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI with its Readiness Assessment Methodology and Ethical Impact Assessment, the OECD AI Principles, the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and 42005, the EU AI Act, the Council of Europe Framework Convention and HUDERIA, OWASP, and the Gulf: Saudi Arabia's SDAIA AI Ethics Principles and data-protection law, and the UAE's federal and DIFC tracks.
- A visual library of plain-language diagrams.
- Four interactive tools: score a use case, classify its risk, draft a one-page policy, and take a readiness snapshot.
- A daily quiz that focuses on your weaker topics.
- A regulatory timeline and a graded source list.
- Progress tracking, with no leaderboards.

## What it's built on

React · TypeScript · a component design system · browser-side interactive tools · a managed backend for optional accounts

## Safeguards

- No account is needed to learn. An optional account only saves progress.
- Analytics are anonymous, with an opt-out.
- Arabic lesson copy is tracked as needing human review before it counts as final.
- The course's own tools (the Anchor Gate and a readiness snapshot) are organisation-level exercises. They are not UNESCO's Readiness Assessment Methodology or Ethical Impact Assessment, which the course teaches separately and credits to UNESCO.
